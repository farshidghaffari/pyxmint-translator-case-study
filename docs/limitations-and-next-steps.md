# Limitations and next steps

## Current status and known limits

PyxMint is a working, field-tested MVP. Approved evidence is qualitative and owner-reported. It does not establish production maturity, commercial traction, paying users, accuracy benchmarks, equal language quality, quantified latency or verified business outcomes.

No causal diagnosis is established for noisy-input or sentence-start behavior. The synthetic/live contrast is not documented as a controlled comparison. Longer-turn observations do not quantify delay. No automated test coverage, deployment/security guarantee, data-retention guarantee or comprehensive browser/device support is claimed.

## PROPOSED NEXT TESTS — not executed

| Dimension | Proposed cases | Proposed observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Language direction | Start with Armenian → Persian, then explicitly enumerate directions among the five listed languages | Sentence-start completeness and reviewer-assessed meaning; no equal-quality presumption |
| Input origin | Matched synthetic material and separately authorized live input | Record the input class without assuming comparability |
| Environment | Quiet and controlled noisy conditions | Record condition and observed behavior; do not infer causality from one attempt |
| Turn length | Define short and longer turns before testing | Conversation continuity and consistently defined timing |
| Transcript | Available workflow with transcript shown or not shown | Whether viewing text helps review; no storage-policy assumption |
| Speaking allowance | Owner-approved synthetic allowance scenarios | Understandability of allowance behavior; no invented limits |

Before execution, define test scripts, repeat counts, review criteria, timing start/end points and privacy requirements. These details are not established results and are deliberately not filled with invented numbers.

## Proposed evidence handling

Use synthetic utterances for shareable examples and label them at creation. Any future real-person testing needs an appropriate consent and privacy plan before collection. Keep raw personal material out of this case study. Publish only separately approved sanitized findings, with methodology and limitations.

## Intended business value

The goal is a more manageable face-to-face multilingual conversation workflow. Reduced friction and operating effort remain intended value, not verified ROI, savings or error reduction. Future business-outcome claims would require a baseline, measurement method, period, scope and disclosure approval.
